# ⚡ BaniTwin: Digital Twin for Underground Power Cables

**Real-Time Thermal State Monitoring & Dynamic Line Rating (DLR) SCADA Operations Dashboard**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

**BaniTwin** is an advanced operational digital twin developed for real-time monitoring and capacity optimization of underground power transmission and distribution cables.

Traditional electric utilities manage underground circuits using fixed, conservative seasonal limits (Static Thermal Ratings). While safe, this static approach creates artificial grid bottlenecks and hides substantial unused transmission capacity.

BaniTwin continuously evaluates ambient environmental factors and operating conditions to determine **Dynamic Thermal Ratings (DTR)**. This allows grid operators to safely unlock **20% to 35% additional transmission throughput** with zero capital infrastructure investment (Zero CapEx), while maintaining full equipment safety and asset longevity.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

- **Real-Time SCADA Control Room Interface:** Designed with a high-contrast industrial theme tailored for transmission and substation dispatch operators.
- **Dynamic Capacity Optimization:** Quantifies and visualizes latent capacity gains over traditional static ampacity caps in real time.
- **Interactive Thermal Gradient Mapping:** Provides a detailed 2D cross-sectional view of heat distribution across the cable layers and surrounding soil.
- **24-Hour Predictive Load Envelope:** Displays a 24-hour diurnal profile comparing active substation loading against dynamic capacity thresholds and traditional static ratings.
- **Interactive SCADA Parameter Control:** Allows operators to test scenarios by adjusting:
  - Active Electrical Load (Amperes)
  - Ambient Soil Temperature
  - Surrounding Soil Thermal Conditions
  - Cable Installation Depth
- **AI-Powered Dispatch Advisory:** Synthesizes real-time status data into actionable recommendations for capacity dispatch and safety compliance.

---

## 💡 Benefits for Utilities & Grid Operators

- **Congestion Relief:** Maximize power flow through existing underground corridors during peak demand periods.
- **Renewable Integration:** Safely absorb variable generation from solar and wind farms without overloading transmission lines.
- **Deferred Capital Expenditure:** Postpone expensive trenching and cable replacement projects by maximizing existing asset utilization.
- **Asset Health Protection:** Ensure operational temperatures remain safely within equipment thermal design limits.
